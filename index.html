At Oscar's Estate, we understand that the journey to your big day is as important as the celebration itself. Our mission is to bring your vision to life, creating a day that is truly remarkable and unforgettable.

The Pavilion has been designed to blend the best of both modern and traditional elements, a unique, stunning venue like no other. With ample space to host larger, multi-cultural pre-nuptial events, alongside the serene beauty of lakes and parkland, our venue offers an atmosphere of tranquility, that is unmatched in the Staffordshire.
