A huge congratulations
on your engagement

At Oscar’s Estate, we understand that the journey to your big day is as important as the celebration itself. Our mission is to bring your vision to life, creating a day that is truly remarkable and unforgettable. Nestled within 140 acres of private parkland and gardens, our luxury wedding venue offers a breathtaking setting for your special day.

Designed to blend the best of both modern and traditional elements, The Pavilion is a unique, stunning venue like no other. With ample space to host larger, multi-cultural pre-nuptial events, alongside the serene beauty of lakes and parkland, our venue offers an atmosphere of tranquillity and grandeur that is unmatched in the Staffordshire district.

The Walled Garden

If the sun is shining on your special day, guests will be directed into the Walled Gardens upon arrival, just a stone’s throw from The Pavilion. The gardens offer a serene and picturesque setting, perfect for your guests to relax while you capture beautiful memories with your photographer. For an extra touch, your suppliers can host your drinks reception and canapés in the Walled Gardens, adding an elegant outdoor element to your celebration.

The Annexe
at The Pavilion

If the weather isn’t kind on your big day, fear not. Once arriving at the venue your guests can spill into the Annexe to enjoy a delicious canapé or beverage at the bar. The Annexe was designed to be a free-flowing room and is the ideal space to host a photobooth or guest book.

Once you are ready for your guests to be seated the curtains can be drawn to make a grand reveal to the main event. The Annexe also serves as an ideal setting for more intimate civil or religious ceremonies.

The Pavilion

Time for the main event! Our Pavilion comfortably hosts over 300 guests, and if you’re expecting even more, we’ve got you covered. What makes us unique is the seamless connection to the Annexe, allowing guests to spread out naturally across both spaces without losing the sense of togetherness.

Our stylish bar and Annexe flow effortlessly from The Pavilion, ensuring guests stay close to the action — no wandering off or missing out on the fun! The beautifully designed bifold doors let you bring the outdoors in, opening up stunning views of the Deer Park and the serene lake beyond — a picture-perfect backdrop for your photographs.

And for that extra special touch, every room is delicately fragranced with the exquisite Nero Bianco range. Curious about our collaboration? Just ask — we’d love to show you how we can tailor it to your day for an unforgettable, personalised experience.

The Cocktail Terrace

A stunning outdoor setting, perfect for dancing, sipping, and mingling under the open sky. As the sun sets, sparkling festoon lights illuminate the terrace, creating a magical atmosphere — ideal for a balmy summer evening. Feeling a bit nippy? No problem! We’ll switch on the outdoor heaters and bring out baskets of Oscar’s cosy blankets to keep everyone warm and comfortable.

Patience Cottage

But it doesn’t stop there — you’ll also have exclusive access to our Patience Cottage, nestled within the serene walled gardens. This charming space is the perfect retreat for brides, grooms, and guests alike. Whether you use it for getting ready before the ceremony, a quiet spot for elderly guests to relax, or even as overnight accommodation, the choice is yours. The cottage is yours to enjoy from 2pm on your wedding day until 10am the following morning, giving you the flexibility and comfort you deserve.

The Pavilion is available on an exclusive use basis, and hire includes:

  • Exclusive use of the Walled Gardens, The Pavilion, Cocktail Terrace and Patience Cottage for 10 hours.
  • The Pavilion is fully insulated, with beams and chandeliers. A/C and Heating in every room.
  • Unlimited Corkage
  • A Wedding co-ordinator to assist/ liaise with your chosen suppliers and be present on the day of your wedding to ensure it runs smoothly.
  • Licenced security staff to ensure safety and security for all your guests.
  • Exclusive use of our Patience cottage. From 2pm on the day of your event until 10am the following day.
  • Parking attendants for when your guests arrive, for a seamless arrival.
  • Commercial kitchen for your caterers to use throughout your event. Our kitchen will have many industrial steel tables and access to hot and cold water.
  • Limewash chivari chairs, elegant and classy without the hassle of hiring chair covers.
  • 5 and 6ft Round tables and trestle tables to suit your bespoke floor/table plans.
  • Square or round cake table and use of our silver Oscars estate cake knife.
  • Easel for your welcome signs/table plans.
  • Skip hire to remove all waste conveniently at the end of your event – the suppliers will thank you for it!
